Physical clocks

Physical clock

Clocks that adhere to the physical time are called physical clocks.

Coordinated Universal Time (UTC):

UTC = TAI (International Atomic Time) + (leap second correction)

TAI: 1s = 9,192,631,770 periods of caesium-133’s resonant frequency

Unix time stamp: the number of seconds that have elapsed since
00:00:00 UTC on 1 January 1970, without adjustments made because of
leap seconds.

▶There is no global clock in a distributed system.
▶Each process has its own internal clock.
▶Clock drift causes the clock error to increase gradually.
▶Clock synchronization is needed to generate reliable timestamps.
