The power of LSH

• In general, one throws items into buckets using several
different “hash functions”.
• You only examine those pairs of items that share a
bucket for at least one of these hashes

• Pros: Designed correctly, only a small fraction of pairs are
ever examined
• Cons: There are false negatives – pairs of similar items that
never even get considered

1. 𝑑𝑑𝑥𝑥, 𝑦𝑦≥0 with 𝑑𝑑(𝑥𝑥, 𝑦𝑦) = 0 if and only if 𝑥𝑥= 𝑦𝑦. (positivity)
